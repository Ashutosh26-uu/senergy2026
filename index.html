About the fest

Where every school of United comes alive.

SYNERGY is the annual fest of United University: engineering, law, design, pharmacy, nursing, hotel management, mass communication, agriculture, commerce and more, all on one stage. It's days of competition, culture and creativity across the Jhalwa campus, ending in a Star Night under the lights.

From "Pulse of United" in 2024 to "Let the Synergy Ignite" in 2025 and 2026, every edition has ended in a packed open-air concert. SYNERGY 2026 brings Sachin-Jigar to the main stage.
