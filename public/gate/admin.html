Add a gate

Two zones. University campus gates (the 3 main gates) and the Fest venue gate are counted separately: getting into the campus does not mark a student as inside the venue. The venue gate admits only students who already checked in at a campus gate, and a campus exit also ends their venue stay.

Entry gates let a pass in only if it is outside. Exit gates let a pass out only if it is inside. So a card can never be used twice in a row, and a student who goes out (exit scan) can come back in.

Each gate gets a secret code, shown once as a QR. Scan it with the guard's phone at . Switching a gate off locks that phone at once and erases the student data on it.

Find a pass

Block: lost card, misuse, or wrong person; it is refused at every gate. Mark outside: for someone who left without an exit scan; their card can then enter once more. For a lost card, block it, then re-issue a new card with the pass maker (--reissue ID) and import the new roster: the old card then shows "OLD PASS".
